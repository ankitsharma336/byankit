:::tip[Engineering Tip]

Always investigate drainage before recommending overlays. Many pavement failures originate from water infiltration rather than insufficient pavement thickness.

:::

:::warning[Field Observation]

Fresh longitudinal cracks near the pavement edge often indicate poor shoulder support or inadequate drainage rather than structural failure alone.

:::

:::danger[Common Mistake]

Do not assume potholes are always caused by traffic loading. Water ingress, poor drainage, and weak subgrade conditions frequently contribute to their formation.
