🛑 Left Zone ($C < C_o$): Lost Time Inefficiency

When cycle length is too short, signal changes occur too frequently. Lost time ($L$) consumes a high percentage of cycle time, causing queue spillback and extreme delay exponential surge.

🎯 Center Zone ($C_o \pm 10\text{s}$): Sweet Spot

The adopted cycle is constrained by the application's selected practical bounds. Verify any project-specific cycle-length limits against the governing IRC provisions and signal-controller requirements.

⏳ Right Zone ($C > C_o$): Long Red Hold Penalty

Excessively long cycles increase uniform delay ($d_1$) because vehicles sit idle during long red intervals. Drivers become impatient, leading to illegal red-light running.
