Guide

How to Use the Concrete Mix Design App

Use this workflow for preliminary mix proportioning and laboratory trial batching.

Back to top ↑
1. Enter Design Parameters

Select concrete grade, exposure, cement, placement method, slump, aggregate size/grading, W/C target, admixture and material specific gravities.

2. Check the Preliminary Mix

Review target mean strength, durability limits, binder content, aggregate proportions and the 1 m³ mix summary on the right.

3. Enter Field Conditions

Enter aggregate absorption and free moisture so the SSD quantities can be converted into practical field batching quantities.

4. Set Trial-Batch Volume

Use the mold estimator or enter a laboratory trial volume directly. Choose Field Corrected or SSD batching basis as appropriate.

5. Review the Audit Log

Open Step 3 to inspect the calculation sequence and assumptions used to produce the mix quantities.

6. Print the Calculation Sheet

Use Print Sheet for a normal multi-page A4 engineering record. Turn on Show References when you want the standards/reference notes included.

Important: This application supports preliminary mix proportioning and trial-batch preparation. Final proportions should be validated through laboratory trials, material test results and the applicable project specifications/codes before field use.
