IS 456 Quick Reference & Assumptions

The app provides focused checks; it is not a substitute for full structural design or approved drawings.

Cl. 26.2.1.1

Development Length

Ld is based on bar stress, diameter and design bond stress. Deformed bars receive the code-specified bond-stress increase; compression receives the specified additional increase.

Cl. 26.2.5.1

Lap Splices

Flexural tension: max(Ld, 30Ø). Direct tension: max(2Ld, 30Ø). Compression: max(Ld, 24Ø). Straight lap length not less than 15Ø or 200 mm.

Cl. 26.4.2 / Table 16

Nominal Cover

Durability cover depends on exposure and is combined with member/bar requirements. Severe/very severe exposure permits a 5 mm reduction for M35 or above. Fire resistance requires a separate Table 16A check.

Table 7 / Cl. 7.1.2

Workability / Slump

Use the tabulated slump ranges as guidance for common placing conditions. Tremie/deep-placement work should be controlled using the specified workability requirements rather than a generic 100–150 mm acceptance band.
