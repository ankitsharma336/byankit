## 💡 Development Philosophy

ByAnkit is being developed around a few simple principles:

**Accuracy before appearance.**

Engineering results should be technically sound before visual refinement.

**Transparency over black-box calculations.**

Users should be able to understand how a result was obtained.

**Standards matter.**

Where applicable, engineering calculations should identify the relevant code, standard, clause, formula, assumption, or limitation.

**Learning through application.**

Engineering tools should also help users understand the concepts behind the calculation.

**Practical over complicated.**

The objective is to make engineering work easier to understand and use.

## ⚠️ Engineering Disclaimer

The engineering tools available through ByAnkit are intended for **educational, reference, checking, and preliminary design purposes**.

They should not replace applicable engineering codes and standards, project specifications, site-specific investigation, detailed analysis, professional engineering judgment, or independent review by a qualified engineer.

Users are responsible for verifying inputs, assumptions, applicable standards, calculations, and final design decisions before using any result for actual engineering or construction work.
